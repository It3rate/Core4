# Core4 OAI 3

A focused rewrite pass for portable units, sensory promotion, and dimensional
commitment.

This document does not replace `core4_cld_v2` yet. It isolates the changes
that now seem most important:

- a resolved unit is portable and can outlive the value that formed it
- repeated use of one unit across many values does not by itself create a
  higher grade
- sequential same-carrier structure and true multi-axis fusion reuse similar
  arithmetic but are not the same kind of structure
- orthogonal promotion should be read as preserved explanatory mismatch, not
  only as geometry
- grade and surviving degrees of freedom are related, but they are not the
  same thing

---

## 1. Portable Units

A unit is the first object in the system that is both:

- internally fixed enough to persist
- reusable across multiple later measurements

Before commitment, a ratio is tied to the episode that formed it. After
commitment, the denominator has become a portable "what is one." The unit
remembers its formation, but it no longer depends on the original value in
order to exist.

This means the same unit can measure many values across its lifetime. That
does not mean it measures all of them at once. Ontologically the unit still
meets values one by one through separate acts of alignment or attachment.

So there are two useful states of the same object:

- **Free unit:** the unit persists, but no value is currently attached
- **Bound measurement:** a value is currently being read through that unit

A free unit is not "nothing." It is latent calibration. Its internal
difference is real, but its contrast is only visible when something attaches
to it and is measured against it.

This is the smallest frame-capable object. More complex structures can also
serve as frames, but the portable unit is the first one that can do so.

## 2. Shared-Unit Families

Once a unit exists, many values may share it across time or across a local
context. This gives a **shared-unit family**:

    { v1/u, v2/u, v3/u, ... }

Operationally that family may be handled as one collection, one frame, or one
batch. The unit has some lifetime, but it will be unchanged during a single
pass across all members.
Under the hood, however, it is still a set of one-to-one comparisons:

    align(v1, u)
    align(v2, u)
    align(v3, u)

The grouping is real, but it is secondary. The primary event remains the
single value meeting the unit. 

This distinction matters because a shared-unit family does not automatically
become a higher-grade object. Reuse is not the same as promotion.

## 3. Sequence Is Not Fusion

There are at least two structurally different ways to combine calibrated
content.

### 3.1 Sequential relation

The same carrier is encountered in ordered episodes:

- previous light and current light
- previous hunger and current hunger
- previous position and current position

The important structure here is **before -> after**. Even if two values are
held at once, they are being understood through ordered encounter. This is
route-like or segment-like structure.

The math may look similar to two-axis composition, but the topology is
different:

- sequence preserves order
- sequence does not automatically provide a co-present interior
- sequence often yields directed change rather than area-like extension

This is the right reading for "normal increase in light" or "recent decrease
in pressure." These are not yet areas. They are calibrated changes in one
carrier.

### 3.2 Fusion

Two relations or units are present with enough independence that they can be
committed into one new "what is one."

Fusion is not just "two things were involved." It requires that the result
support a stable joint reading that survives the original episode. If the
joint structure supports meaningful interior positions or stable cross-sections,
then a higher-grade entity has appeared.

So sequence and fusion may share arithmetic patterns, but they differ in what
kind of object survives.

## 4. Grade Versus Degrees of Freedom

Grade is about structural composition.

Degrees of freedom are about what independent adjustments remain available
after commitment.

These are related but not identical.

### 4.1 Grade

Grade tracks how many layers of opposition and composition were needed to form
the object.

- grade 0: atomic value/unit pair
- grade 1: one opposed relation over grade 0
- grade 2: one opposed relation over grade 1

### 4.2 Surviving freedom

An object may be built from two axes or two components while exposing only one
ordinary adjustable value after commitment.

This is why units like miles-per-hour or pounds-per-square-inch feel unusual.
They are not fake fusions, and they are not half-grades. They are better read
as **asymmetric fusions**:

- they have more than one contributor in formation
- one contributor is locked into the unit more strongly than the other
- the resolved object exposes only one ordinary free value in normal use

Area is a **symmetric fusion**. Width and height are interchangeable within
the square unit.

Speed or pressure is an **asymmetric fusion**. The denominator side is not
freely dragged around in the same way during ordinary use.

So the right distinction is not "grade 1.5." The better distinction is:

- structural grade
- symmetric versus asymmetric fusion
- number of surviving degrees of freedom

## 5. Orthogonality as Explanatory Mismatch

Orthogonality should not be restricted to "another geometric axis."

The deeper meaning is:

- some structured variation is real
- the current carrier or frame cannot honestly absorb it
- the mismatch persists
- the system preserves that mismatch instead of discarding it

That preserved mismatch is orthogonal in the important sense.

Opposed sign is the first and simplest local case. But it is not the whole
story.

Examples:

- a light signal is partly contaminated by heat
- a motion rule based on light predicts one food outcome, but the actual food
  return is consistently different
- a pressure reading carries a residual that tracks vibration instead of
  pressure itself

In each case, the current measurement scheme is getting structured residue it
cannot explain. If the residue is weak or inconsistent, it remains tension. If
it repeatedly helps reduce future mismatch, it becomes a candidate for a new
association or a new unit family.

Orthogonality is therefore about preserved non-assimilation, not about a
pre-stated extra axis.

## 6. From Tension to Association to Commitment

Not every mismatch should immediately become a new dimension. There is an
important ladder:

1. **Residual tension**
   Some information does not fit the current frame.

2. **Repeated correlation**
   The same kind of residue keeps appearing with some other signal or outcome.

3. **Weak association**
   The system begins reusing one signal to partially absorb another mismatch.
   This is not yet a true unit.

4. **Committed association**
   The relation persists strongly enough to serve as a reusable calibration.
   Now it behaves more like a unit or axis than like a coincidence.

5. **Independent reuse**
   The committed relation can calibrate later cases beyond the original
   episode. At this point a true promoted structure exists.

This ladder matters for both temporal learning and cross-sensory separation.
It also gives a natural way for weak bonds to dissolve:

- if later episodes keep supporting the association, the bond strengthens
- if the signals stop helping explain each other, the bond weakens
- below some useful threshold, the commitment should release

So commitment is not merely co-occurrence. It is persistent reduction of
structured mismatch.

## 7. A Better Reading of the Sensory Ladder

The sensory story can now be stated more cleanly.

### 7.1 Raw extent

A creature receives raw light. This is only "some amount."

There is no calibrated scale yet, no stable unit, and no preserved baseline.
The signal may drive behavior, but it is not yet measurement in the stronger
sense.

### 7.2 Transient directional change

If the creature can tell that the reading is currently going up or down, it
has more than raw amount, but still not a portable measure of "how much" in a
stable comparative sense.

This is an encounter with change, but not yet a committed change unit.

### 7.3 Preserved baseline unit

Once one extent is committed as a unit and preserved, later light values can
be read as:

- lighter than normal
- darker than normal
- equal to normal

This is the first portable calibration.

### 7.4 Sequential grade-1 change

Now take two light readings under the same preserved light unit:

- previous light
- current light

This yields a directed relation of change. The important content is ordered:
from previous to current. The result is still same-carrier structure. It is
best read as grade 1.

This allows:

- getting brighter
- getting darker
- recent increase size
- recent decrease size

### 7.5 Preserved unit of normal change

If the system repeatedly encounters light changes, it may commit a portable
unit for typical change. That unit is still about one carrier, but now it
calibrates changes rather than raw levels.

This means the system can distinguish:

- a small increase
- a large increase
- a small decrease
- a large decrease

This is still not the same as a true two-dimensional interior. It is a
preserved calibration for directed change in one carrier.

### 7.6 Feedback mismatch

Suppose the light signal predicts one level of food or energy return, but the
actual return is consistently off. The excess does not vanish. It persists as
tension.

At first this tension may simply bias behavior:

- ramp motion when light rises sharply
- become still when light drops

But if the unresolved part keeps correlating with some additional factor, the
system has found a candidate orthogonal explanation.

### 7.7 Cross-sensory separation

If the light sensor is also weakly responding to heat, the system may first
treat the mixture as one carrier. Over repeated episodes, however, some
residual mismatch may be better explained by separating:

- light-like contribution
- heat-like contribution

That separation is not granted in advance. It is discovered because the mixed
signal keeps failing to settle as one thing.

This is the non-geometric reading of dimensional promotion: the system commits
a new explanatory axis because repeated structured mismatch demands it.

## 8. What Counts as Grade 2

A useful current rule is:

- grade 1 is enough for directed change in one carrier
- grade 2 begins when two grade-1 relations are fused into one committed
  structure with its own portable calibration

One heuristic is:

> A grade-2 unit can yield meaningful grade-1 slices or projections without
> collapsing back into unrelated raw episodes.

That matches the intuition that a grade-2 object can generate grade-1 views
out of its own unit structure.

Examples that may count as grade 2:

- a stable light/heat separation with its own reusable calibration
- a fused change/change relation where two independent change axes survive
- a genuine area-like or field-like entity with meaningful interior variation

Examples that do not yet count:

- one light baseline used across many readings
- previous and current light under one shared unit
- a temporary association that has not yet committed

## 9. The Role of Sign and Unit State

The sign patterns still matter. So does whether a denominator is settled.

The following distinctions remain important:

- aligned measurement: `n / +u`
- opposed measurement: `n / -u` at the local reading level
- unresolved measurement: `n / 0`
- composite dominant/recessive ordering at higher grades

But these do not decide the full ontology alone. To know what kind of object
is being formed, we also need:

- whether the relation is sequential or co-present
- whether the mismatch is temporary or committed
- whether a joint denominator survives
- how many degrees of freedom remain after commitment

So sign is one ingredient in promotion, not the only one.

## 10. Proposed Terms for the Next Rewrite

These terms seem worth stabilizing in the next full document:

- **Portable unit:** the first persistent calibrator
- **Free unit:** a portable unit with no current value attached
- **Bound measurement:** a value currently read through a unit
- **Shared-unit family:** many values linked by one denominator
- **Sequential relation:** ordered same-carrier structure
- **Symmetric fusion:** a fusion whose contributors are interchangeable in use
- **Asymmetric fusion:** a fusion whose contributors are not interchangeable in
  ordinary use
- **Residual tension:** structured mismatch not yet explained
- **Weak association:** reusable but uncommitted mismatch reduction
- **Committed association:** stable enough to behave like a new unit or axis

These are naming proposals, not final axioms.

---

## 11. Current Direction

The updated picture is:

- units are not permanently glued to their original values
- a portable unit is the first real frame-capable object
- families of values can share one unit without creating a higher grade
- temporal or ordered change should usually be read as sequential grade-1
  structure
- new dimensions should be read as committed explanatory relations, not merely
  as geometric directions
- grade must stay distinct from the number of freedoms exposed in ordinary use

This preserves the Core4 emphasis on structure, tension, and lawful
construction from experience, while giving a more physical account of how
measurement and dimensional promotion can arise from sensory life.
